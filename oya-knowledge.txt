## JEWELLERY CARE
==================================================

To maintain jewellery:

• Store in a dry place.
• Keep away from perfumes and chemicals.
• Remove before swimming or bathing.
• Clean gently using a soft cloth.
• Store separately to avoid scratches.
• Avoid direct contact with lotions and cosmetics.

Store it safely in a dry place, avoid exposure to perfumes and harsh chemicals, clean with a soft cloth, and keep each piece separately.
